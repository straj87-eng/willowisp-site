A no-snooze alarm that makes you get out of bed

Will-O-Wisp has no snooze button and no off button by your bed. It only stops when you walk to tags (NFC stickers, QR codes or barcodes) placed around your home.

Why ordinary alarms fail

Most alarms make two assumptions: that you'll hear them, and that once you hear them you'll get up. The second one is the problem. If the off switch is within reach, a half-asleep brain will find it. Many people switch off alarms without even remembering doing it.

Snooze makes it worse. Each snooze gives you a few minutes of broken, light sleep, and teaches you that the first alarm doesn't really count.

What a no-snooze alarm needs to do

  • No snooze button. Not hidden, not limited: none.
  • No off switch near the bed. The decision to get up has to happen on your feet, not under the blanket.
  • No easy escape. Restarting the phone or switching it off shouldn't end the alarm.

Will-O-Wisp does all three. You swipe once to quiet it, and then it guides you to your first tag. If you don't reach it within 20 seconds, it rings again. If you restart the phone, it rings again after the restart.

Five small wins instead of one big fight

"Just get up" is a big decision for a sleepy brain. Walking to the door is a small one. Will-O-Wisp breaks the morning into a short route of tags; each one is easy, and by the last one you're awake:

  1. Bedroom door: you're out of bed. That's the hardest win, done.
  2. Fish tank: a small job (feed them) that needs your eyes open.
  3. Stair corner: your body is moving now.
  4. Mushroom painting: lights on, eyes properly open.
  5. Sink: brushing. By now you're awake, and the alarm stops.

Gentle, not cruel

A no-snooze alarm doesn't have to be loud or violent. Will-O-Wisp can glow before it rings (sunrise mode), starts quietly and gets louder, and in Normal mode goes quiet while you walk. There's also a vibrate-only Quiet mode for shared rooms.

Questions

Can I turn snooze back on?

No. That's the point of the app. If you need a nap alarm or a reminder, the app also has plain alarms without tags.

What if I'm ill or travelling?

You can switch an alarm off, but that day shows in orange on your calendar. Streak freezes (Pro) protect your streak twice a month, and Travel tag sets let you use QR codes in a hotel.

Is it safe to rely on for flights?

It's built to be reliable, but for anything critical, set a backup alarm as well.
