An alarm app for heavy sleepers who switch alarms off in their sleep

If you sleep through alarms, or switch them off without remembering, the problem usually isn't volume. It's that the off switch is next to you. Will-O-Wisp moves the off switch to tags around your home.

Louder isn't the answer

Heavy sleepers try louder alarms, more alarms and the phone across the room. Louder alarms still get switched off. More alarms train you to ignore alarms. And the phone across the room gets you up for exactly the twelve steps it takes to walk there and back to bed.

What works is making the off switch something you can only reach once you're out of the bedroom, and then keeping you moving long enough to wake up properly.

Settings for really heavy sleepers

  • Crazy mode: keeps ringing all the way to your last tag instead of going quiet while you walk.
  • Random order: your tags shuffle every day, so you can't do the route on autopilot.
  • Restart-proof: switching the phone off or restarting it doesn't end the alarm.
  • Put the first tag outside the bedroom, ideally on the door or in the hall.
  • Sunrise mode: the screen and torch glow before the alarm, so you wake into light.

A route, not a sprint

The goal isn't to scare you awake. It's a short chain of easy steps that ends with you upright and alert. Each tag is a small win:

  1. Bedroom door: you're out of bed. That's the hardest win, done.
  2. Fish tank: a small job (feed them) that needs your eyes open.
  3. Stair corner: your body is moving now.
  4. Mushroom painting: lights on, eyes properly open.
  5. Sink: brushing. By now you're awake, and the alarm stops.

If you still struggle

Sleeping through very loud alarms regularly, or feeling exhausted despite enough hours in bed, can have medical causes such as sleep apnoea. If that sounds like you, it's worth talking to a doctor. An alarm helps you get up; it can't fix poor sleep.

Questions

Will it wake me if I sleep through sound?

It uses sound, vibration and light (sunrise mode), and it keeps ringing until you reach your tags. In Crazy mode it rings until the last one.

I share a room. Will it wake my partner?

Quiet mode is vibrate-only with the same walk-to-your-tags rules.

Does it work if I switch off my phone?

Yes, with restart-proof (Pro): the alarm rings again after the phone restarts, even before you unlock it.
