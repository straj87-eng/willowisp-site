A QR code alarm app that makes you get up to scan

Will-O-Wisp stops only when you scan the QR codes (or barcodes) you've placed around your home. It works on every Android phone with a camera, no NFC needed, and the app gives you 5 printable QR codes for free.

Swipe the story →

How it works

  1. In the app, open Tags → Get 5 printable QR codes. You get an A5 PDF with cut lines.
  2. Print, cut, and stick the codes along your morning: bedroom door, bathroom, kitchen, wherever you want to walk to.
  3. Scan each one once to register it, in the order you'll walk.
  4. Set your alarm. When it rings, swipe once, then walk and scan each code. The alarm stops at the last one.

Use barcodes you already own

No printer? Any product barcode works as a tag. Put your toothpaste at the sink and register its barcode: by the time the alarm stops, you're already brushing.

Why a route, not one code

One code across the room gets you up, but you can scan it and go back to bed. A route of several codes turns waking up into a few small, easy wins. By the last one you're properly awake. Here is a real route:

  1. Bedroom door: you're out of bed. That's the hardest win, done.
  2. Fish tank: a small job (feed them) that needs your eyes open.
  3. Stair corner: your body is moving now.
  4. Mushroom painting: lights on, eyes properly open.
  5. Sink: brushing. By now you're awake, and the alarm stops.

Tips for codes that scan every time

  • Stick codes at chest height where there's some light (the app has a torch button for dark rooms).
  • Keep them flat and away from glare; matte paper scans better than glossy.
  • Don't put the first code in the bedroom. Put it on the door or outside, so the first win is leaving the bed.

Questions

Do I need NFC for a QR code alarm?

No. QR codes and barcodes use the camera, so they work on any Android phone with a camera.

Can I scan a photo of the QR code instead of walking?

Keep the only copy of each code where it is stuck. If you keep a photo by the bed, you're only cheating yourself; Random order and Crazy mode make cheating harder.

Is it free?

Every new account gets 15 days of full Pro free, no card. The free version after that keeps 1 tag; Pro allows up to 10.
