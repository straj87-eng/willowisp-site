Why moving works better than willpower

Lying down, you can argue with any alarm. Standing up and walking, the argument is over. A short walk gets you upright, gets your blood moving and puts you in a lit room — and most people find the grogginess fades fast once they're on their feet.

Will-O-Wisp is built around that one idea. There's no snooze and no button to tap from bed. The alarm only stops when you've walked to every tag, so the first thing you do each morning is move.

Why five tags?

  • About 2–3 minutes of walking — enough to wake your body, not a chore.
  • Different rooms — you actually leave the bedroom.
  • Hard to cheat — five stickers can't all sit within reach of your bed.
  • A spare built in — pause a damaged tag and you still have four.
  • The sweet spot — two is too easy, ten gets annoying on a rushed morning.

Questions

What is an NFC tag?

An NFC tag is a thin sticker, about the size of a coin, with a tiny chip and antenna inside. It has no battery and never needs charging. When you hold your phone close to it (about 1–3 cm), the phone powers it for a split second and reads its unique code — the same tap technology as contactless cards and metro cards. Will-O-Wisp simply remembers each sticker's code, so the alarm knows which one you tapped. Nothing is written to the sticker and no internet is needed. They come in packs and cost very little each.

Which NFC tags do I need?

NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 stickers all work, as do most other NFC stickers and key fobs. They are cheap and widely sold online. Stick them on wood, plastic, glass or painted walls — not directly on metal (fridges, kettles, steel frames), which blocks NFC unless the tag is sold as "anti-metal". Five tags is a good start; the app links to recommended ones for your country.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Will-O-Wisp launches first on Android phones with NFC. An iPhone version is planned.

What if I'm travelling?

Keep a second set of tags in your bag and choose the Travel set for that night's alarm, or simply leave the alarm off. The alarm switches itself off after each morning, so it never rings by surprise.

Will it ring if my phone is on silent?

Yes. It uses your phone's alarm channel and turns the alarm volume up while ringing. Follow the setup steps in the app so battery saving doesn't block it.
