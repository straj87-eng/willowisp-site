Three steps to a morning that sticks

No snooze button, no shaking your phone in bed. Just a short walk that wakes your body before your brain can argue.

  1. Stick your tags

    Place cheap NFC stickers where you need to be in the morning — the bathroom mirror, the kettle, the front door. Name each one in the app.

  2. Arm one alarm

    One alarm, set each night. A bedtime reminder nudges you to switch it on and tells you how much sleep you'll get.

  3. Walk the trail

    When it rings, the app tells you which tag is next. Tap them in order and the wisp swirls into a golden finish.

Choose how hard to be on yourself

Both modes need every tag. The difference is how much noise you get on the way.

Normal mode

Each correct tap silences the alarm. You have 20 seconds to reach the next tag before it starts ringing again.

For gentle, steady mornings.

Crazy mode

It keeps ringing the whole way. Only the last tag brings silence. Random order makes sure you can't plan a shortcut.

For the truly committed sleepers.

Questions

Which NFC tags do I need?

Any NFC sticker works. NTAG213 or NTAG215 stickers are cheap, reliable and widely sold online. Five tags is a good start; the app links to recommended ones for your country.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Will-O-Wisp launches first on Android phones with NFC. An iPhone version is planned.

What if I'm travelling?

Keep a second set of tags in your bag and choose the Travel set for that night's alarm, or simply leave the alarm off. The alarm switches itself off after each morning, so it never rings by surprise.

Will it ring if my phone is on silent?

Yes. It uses your phone's alarm channel and turns the alarm volume up while ringing. Follow the setup steps in the app so battery saving doesn't block it.

What happens to my data?

Your alarm, tags, streaks and badges stay on your phone. We only receive your email if you choose to join our tips and offers list. Read the privacy policy.
