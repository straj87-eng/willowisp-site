Looking for an Alarmy alternative? Here's how Will-O-Wisp is different

Alarmy is a popular alarm app with "missions" such as taking a photo, scanning a barcode, solving maths or shaking the phone. Will-O-Wisp takes a narrower approach: a short route of tags around your home that walks you from bed to fully awake.

Both apps aim at the same problem: getting you out of bed instead of snoozing. Which one suits you depends on how you like to wake up. This page describes Will-O-Wisp's approach; check the current version of any app before choosing, because features change.

Choose Will-O-Wisp if…

  • You switch off alarms in your sleep and want the off switch outside your bedroom.
  • You'd rather wake up gradually through a short walk than through a puzzle in bed.
  • You want to build a morning routine, with each tag on a habit (water, brushing, lights).

Another app may suit you better if…

  • You want lots of different mission types, or you're on iPhone (Will-O-Wisp is Android only for now).

Questions

Is Will-O-Wisp free?

Every new account gets 15 days of full Pro, no card. After that the free version keeps 1 tag, Normal mode and streaks; Pro adds up to 10 tags and the anti-cheat features.

Can I use barcodes like in other alarm apps?

Yes. Any product barcode or QR code can be a tag, and NFC stickers work too.
