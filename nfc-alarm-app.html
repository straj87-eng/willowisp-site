An NFC alarm app you can't switch off from bed

Will-O-Wisp is an Android alarm that stops only when you tap your phone on NFC stickers placed around your home. There's no snooze button and no off button by the bed, so you have to get up and walk.

Swipe the story →

How an NFC alarm works

An NFC tag is a small sticker with a chip inside. Your phone reads it when you hold it close, the same way contactless payment works, but the tag holds no money and no personal data. Will-O-Wisp remembers each tag you register. When the alarm rings, the only way to stop it is to reach those tags and tap them.

Stick the tags out of reach of your bed: the bedroom door, the bathroom, the kitchen. Your sleepy hand can't switch the alarm off any more. Your legs have to do it.

Why several tags work better than one

A single tag gets you out of bed, but it's easy to tap it and crawl back. Will-O-Wisp lets you build a route of tags, and each tap is a small win. This is the route the developer uses every morning:

  1. Bedroom door: you're out of bed. That's the hardest win, done.
  2. Fish tank: a small job (feed them) that needs your eyes open.
  3. Stair corner: your body is moving now.
  4. Mushroom painting: lights on, eyes properly open.
  5. Sink: brushing. By now you're awake, and the alarm stops.

Getting up is too big to win in one go at 5:30 AM. Walking to the door isn't. Then the fish. Then the stairs. That's the whole idea: Wake. Walk. Win.

Which NFC tags to buy

  • NTAG213 stickers are the cheapest and work perfectly. NTAG215 and NTAG216 work too.
  • Round stickers about 25–30 mm across are easy to stick on doors, frames and shelves.
  • Avoid sticking them directly on metal; use an on-metal tag there.

No NFC on your phone?

Many phones don't have NFC. Will-O-Wisp also accepts QR codes and any product barcode (a toothpaste tube, a cereal box) as tags. The app gives you a free printable sheet of 5 QR codes.

What else the app does

  • Swipe to wake: swipe once, the alarm goes quiet and a voice tells you the next tag. You have 20 seconds to reach it or it rings again.
  • Crazy mode keeps ringing until the last tag, for very heavy sleepers.
  • Random order shuffles your tags so you can't sleepwalk the route.
  • Rings after a restart, even before the phone is unlocked.
  • Streaks and a habit calendar, so you can see your mornings add up.

Questions

Does an NFC alarm app work on iPhone?

Will-O-Wisp is Android only for now.

Is NFC safe? Can the tag read my phone?

Yes, it's safe. The sticker only holds a small ID that the app recognises. It can't read your phone, payments or personal data.

Does the alarm still ring if my phone is on silent or battery saver?

Yes. It uses Android's alarm channel, which rings in silent mode, and it's built to ring on battery saver and after a restart.

How many tags can I use?

Every new account gets 15 days of full Pro, with up to 10 tags. After that, the free version keeps 1 tag; Pro keeps up to 10.
