The app

The app is an alarm that you stop by tapping NFC tags with your phone. It needs an Android phone with NFC and NFC tags, which are not supplied with the app. Any brand of compatible NFC sticker can be used.

Please read: alarm reliability

We work hard to make the alarm dependable, but an app on a phone cannot be guaranteed to ring every time. The alarm can be affected by your phone's battery-saving settings, manufacturer restrictions, operating-system updates, low battery, the phone being switched off, muted or out of storage, and other factors outside our control. Please follow the setup steps in the app and test your alarm.

Safety

Place tags where you can reach them safely, and take care when walking in the dark. Don't place tags near stairs, wet floors or hazards. Sunrise mode can turn on the flashlight — don't look directly into it.
